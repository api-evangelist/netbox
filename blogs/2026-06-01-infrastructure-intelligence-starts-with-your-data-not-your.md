Most enterprises struggle to understand their own infrastructure due to fragmented point solutions that create "dark data" and cost $230K to $1.6M annually in overlapping tools. According to HyperFRAME Research, over 70% of industry leaders identify data quality as the primary constraint limiting their AI capabilities. NetBox Labs advocates shifting from passive inventory systems to an active unified platform featuring automated drift detection, observability, and agentic AI workflows to achieve true infrastructure intelligence.
